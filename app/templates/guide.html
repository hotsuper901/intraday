How to trade the signal manual for Binomo · Pocket Option · IQ Option · Quotex · Olymp Trade · Deriv · ExpertOption and any MT5/MT4 broker

1 · Reading the signal panel

Every ticker page (/ticker/SYMBOL) has a Trading signal panel built from 1-minute + 5-minute bars with nine indicators (EMA stack, MACD, RSI, stochastic, Bollinger, VWAP, OBV, ATR, candlestick patterns). It refreshes every 30 seconds and re-runs on the ⟳ Analyze button.

STRONG BUYscore ≥ +3.0 BUYscore ≥ +1.2 NEUTRAL−1.2 … +1.2 SELLscore ≤ −1.2 STRONG SELLscore ≤ −3.0
  • Verdict — the headline call. Trade only BUY / STRONG BUY / SELL / STRONG SELL. Skip NEUTRAL entirely.
  • Confidence — 45–94%. Under 60 treat the signal as noise. Over 75 with confluence is the sweet spot.
  • 1m+5m confluence — both timeframes agree and are strong. This is the highest-quality setup on the panel.
  • 1m / 5m boxes — each timeframe's own verdict, score, EMA state, RSI, stochastic and pattern. If they conflict (1m SELL, 5m BUY), the combined call is NEUTRAL — that is the panel telling you no trade.
  • Prediction — Entry = current price. Target = nearest resistance/support (or 3× ATR). Stop = ATR/S&R based. R:R = reward/risk ratio; only take signals where the panel shows roughly 1 : 1.5 or better.
  • Chips — RSI, MACD, Stoch, EMA, VWAP, BB, pattern values behind the score.
  • 5m-only analysis chip — 1-minute data was unavailable; the signal is a single-timeframe call. Halve your normal size.

2 · The 5-step trade routine

1
Open the screener, filter by movers (Min chg % 1.5, Min rel vol 1.5), click a symbol — or open /ticker/EURUSD=X directly for the pair you trade.
2
Wait for the current 5-minute candle to close. The signal recalculates on the closed bar; entries taken mid-candle get invalidated by the next refresh.
3
Only act on BUY/SELL or stronger, confidence ≥ 60, and check the 1m/5m boxes agree in direction (confluence label visible).
4
Confirm the same asset on your broker's chart — same pair, same direction, and ideally your broker's own momentum (price above VWAP on the panel for buys).
5
Enter with the platform you use (below). The signal is valid for the current 5-minute bar — if the next bar changes the verdict, stand down and wait for the next setup.

4 · Binomo

Fixed-time trades on FX majors · EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY … map 1:1 to the EURUSD=X style pairs

  • Open the pair that matches the ticker — EURUSD=X → EUR/USD, GBPJPY=X → GBP/JPY, BTC-USD → BTC/USD (Binomo lists it as BTC/USD or Bitcoin).
  • Set the chart to 5-minute candles and expiry to 5 min (BUY/SELL) or 10–15 min (STRONG with confluence).
  • Entry at the close of the 5m candle: HIGH if the panel says BUY, LOW if SELL. If price hits the panel's stop level before expiry, the setup failed — do not double down.
  • OTC pairs: the signal still works but spreads vary; prefer the standard FX pairs. Skip trading 3 minutes either side of high-impact news (NFP, CPI, FOMC).
  • Practice on the free $1,000 demo until you've seen 30+ signal setups play out; then start with minimum $1 trades.
Binomo payouts are fixed at entry and losses are 100% of stake — the signal's R:R can't save a losing binary. That's why the NEUTRAL skip and the confidence ≥ 60 filter matter more than the verdict itself.

5 · Pocket Option

High/Low + OTC · 100+ assets, 5s–4h expiry, up to 92% payout

  • Match symbols: AAPL → Apple, BTC-USD → BTC/USD, EURUSD=X → EUR/USD; add gold to the watchlist as GC=F for gold signals.
  • Use standard High/Low, not Turbo, with 5 min expiry for BUY/SELL and 15 min for STRONG+confluence.
  • Check the pair's payout before entry — below 80% on a 5m expiry, skip it; the signal needs the payout to cover losing streaks.
  • The R:R line in the prediction is your stake-sizing guide: treat target/stop as the price range you expect within the expiry; if the expected move (target) is tiny relative to the stop, the signal is saying the range is tight — skip.
  • If you trade Pocket Option's forex CFD mode, apply the prediction literally: entry at price, TP at target, SL at stop.

6 · IQ Option

Regulated broker · FX CFDs, digital options, and (jurisdiction-dependent) binaries

  • FX CFD mode is where this signal fits best: set a market order on EUR/USD, GBP/JPY etc., TP = panel target, SL = panel stop. The R:R shown is your actual risk/reward.
  • Digital options: 1-minute and 5-minute expiries only when the signal is STRONG with confluence; use 3–5 minute expiry at the 5m candle close.
  • Leverage note: with 1:30 retail leverage, one losing signal at full size is a large drawdown — size so the SL distance equals ≤ 1–2% of account (account × risk% ÷ stop distance = units).
  • IQ Option uses real market execution — a signal is an edge, not an instruction; slippage on news candles can move your fill past the stop.

8 · Risk rules (non-negotiable)

  • Risk 1–2% of the account per trade (5% absolute max, STRONG signals only).
  • Max 3 consecutive losses per session — then stop for the day. Signals don't revenge-trade; you do.
  • Never martingale. Doubling after a loss is how accounts die; the signal already gave you a stop level.
  • Skip the 3 minutes before/after high-impact news (NFP, CPI, FOMC, rate decisions). The panel can't see the event, and spreads explode.
  • One open trade per signal. Stacking 5 same-direction bets on one bar is one trade with extra steps and worse fills.
  • Trade the signal on the demo account of every platform for at least a week before real money — this manual is the theory, the demo is the practice.
No signal predicts the future — this panel measures momentum, trend and volatility, and even a 94%-confidence STRONG call loses sometimes. Treat the signal as a filter that keeps you out of bad entries, not as a guarantee.
